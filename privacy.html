Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy covers the Kuchi Mama uploader (Google Cloud project "kuchimama25"), a private tool used by the owner of the Kuchi Mama YouTube channel to publish and review the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests these permissions through the YouTube API Services:

  • youtube.upload – upload videos to the owner's channel.
  • youtube – set titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and publish times on the owner's videos.
  • yt-analytics.readonly – read view and engagement reports for the owner's channel.

When the account owner signs in with Instagram, the app requests these permissions through the Instagram API:

  • instagram_business_basic – read the owner's account name and ID.
  • instagram_business_content_publish – publish Reels to the owner's own Instagram account.

To post a Reel, the video file is made reachable through a temporary link only while Instagram downloads it; the link is closed straight afterwards.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the channel's videos and to read its performance so future videos can be planned. The app does not read, collect or store data about viewers or any other YouTube or Instagram users.

Storage

The sign-in token and analytics reports are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sold, shared with or sent to third parties, and they are not used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data is deleted when access is revoked or on request.

For Instagram, remove the app under Instagram Settings → Apps and websites.

Deleting your data

To have all data the app holds deleted (sign-in tokens and upload logs on the owner's computer), revoke access as above or email srini2dl@gmail.com. It is deleted within 7 days and confirmed by email.

Instagram and Meta terms

Instagram posting follows the Instagram Terms of Use and Meta Platform Terms.

Google and YouTube terms

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. See also the Kuchi Mama Terms of Service.
